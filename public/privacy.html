Privacy

WrongBot has no accounts, no logins, and no forms that collect personal information. This page covers everything the site does that touches a visitor's data.

Analytics

This site uses Google Analytics (gtag.js) to see aggregate traffic — roughly how many people visit, what pages they read, and where they came from. Google Analytics sets cookies and processes standard web analytics data (IP-derived approximate location, browser/device type, pages viewed) under Google's own privacy terms; see Google's Privacy Policy for what Google itself does with that data.

This domain is served through Cloudflare, which automatically injects its own first-party Web Analytics beacon (static.cloudflareinsights.com) and runs Zaraz, Cloudflare's tag manager, at the account/zone level shared with the rest of jasperkooij.com — not something configured per-page in this repository. See Cloudflare's Privacy Policy for what Cloudflare itself does with that data.

No other tracking, advertising, or fingerprinting scripts run on this site.

The MCP tool and chat widget

The chat widget on the homepage runs entirely in your browser — the questions you type into it never leave your device. The MCP tool (get_wrong_answer), when called by an AI client you connect, receives only the question text sent by that client for that single request; WrongBot does not log, store, or retain tool-call content.

Third parties

The "Buy Me a Coffee" badge in the footer links out to buymeacoffee.com; that site's own privacy policy applies once you click it. This site does not embed any third-party widget beyond Google Analytics and that outbound link.
